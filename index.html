Development Economist

Joël
Cariolle

Research Officer at FERDI and Affiliate Researcher at CERDI — University Clermont-Auvergne, where I lead FERDI's programme on digital transformation. Unlike the ecological transition, digitalization is not a chosen destination but a process that imposes itself on economies — all the more so in the age of artificial intelligence and of asymmetric power between states and Big Tech. My research traces these digitalization trajectories in the developing world, from the rollout of internet and submarine-cable infrastructure to its double potential: an accelerator of development through trade, financial inclusion and new livelihoods, but also a source of inequality, dependence and new digital vulnerabilities. My earlier work spans economic vulnerability in Least Developed Countries, regional integration in West Africa, and corruption in developing economies.
